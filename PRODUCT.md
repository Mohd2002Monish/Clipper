# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Content creators, social publishers, and video automation managers needing fast, streamlined tools to split, brand, reframe, schedule, and track YouTube videos without friction.

## Product Purpose
VideoTimmer automates the entire social video lifecycle: importing from Google Drive, reframing to 9:16 Shorts or 16:9 Long-Form, automated rendering, automated hourly scheduling (up to 8/day), and live YouTube views tracking.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, precise, functional, modern. Clear hierarchy with calm, confident execution.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered dashboards with endless nested cards and redundant duplicate metrics.
- Saturated AI defaults (unnecessary emojis, neon gradients, bloated text descriptions).
- Cluttered side-by-side graphs competing for attention.

## Design Principles
1. **At-a-Glance Clarity:** Every page must present its primary action and data instantly without requiring scroll or manual configuration.
2. **Breathable Spacing:** 4/8px spacing rhythm with generous padding and crisp hairline borders rather than heavy drop shadows.
3. **Clean Vector Precision:** Exclusively Lucide SVG vector icons, zero emojis, clean sans-serif typography.
4. **Immediate Feedback:** Instant visual response for every interactive button, tab, and toggle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliance across light and dark surfaces, high-contrast text ratios (≥4.5:1), and semantic touch/click targets (≥44px).
